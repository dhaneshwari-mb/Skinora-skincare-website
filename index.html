What is your skin type?

Choose the option that best describes how your skin feels most of the time.

Normal

Balanced moisture, minimal sensitivity, rarely feels too oily or dry.

Dry

Feels tight, flaky, dull, or rough, especially after cleansing.

Oily

Excess shine all over, enlarged pores, prone to breakouts.

Combination

Oily T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) with normal or dry cheeks.

Sensitive

Easily turns red, stings, reacts to products, flush or itchy.
